# Course II exam · Robot Autonomy

**Covers:** Modules 4–5 and the capstone, drawing on Courses I throughout · **Format:** cross-module, closed-book · **Time:** ~60 min · **Passing bar:** 80%

Course I's exam asked whether you understood the pieces. This one asks whether you can reason about a *system* — because that's what Modules 4 and 5 added. Most questions here deliberately span two or three modules, and several are drawn from real failures documented in the [capstone engineering log](capstone-log.md).

Take it closed-book after you've run the capstone yourself. Scoring under 80% is useful information rather than a problem: the explanations name the lesson to revisit.
